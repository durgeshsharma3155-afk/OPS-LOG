001 — What it is

What is Ops Log?

Ops Log is a free personal execution tracker. Instead of picking from templates, you describe what you're working on in a few rough sentences — business, fitness, studies, anything — and it builds a tracker specifically around that: your actual goals, a real day-count to your actual deadline, daily tasks, and a schedule to hold yourself to.

002 — Cost

Is it free?

Yes. You sign in with Google and start tracking immediately — no subscription required for the core tracker.

003 — The difference

How is it different from other habit trackers?

Most habit trackers hand you a fixed template and a generic 30, 60, or 90-day challenge. Ops Log builds the tracker from what you actually write about your own goals, and calculates a real countdown based on your actual deadline — mention a goal tied to a specific age or date, and it computes the real number of days remaining instead of defaulting to a generic length.

004 — Who it's for

Who is it for?

Working individuals actively pursuing a goal — founders, students, athletes, creators, or anyone balancing multiple areas of their life who wants one place to track execution across all of them.

005 — Accuracy

What if it gets something wrong?

Before anything is finalized, Ops Log shows you exactly what it built and lets you adjust any part of it — the goals, the timeline, or the schedule — so it always ends up accurate to what you actually meant.

006 — Devices

Does it work across multiple devices?

Yes. Signing in with the same Google account syncs your setup across devices.

007 — Privacy

What happens to my data?

Your email and name are stored to sync your setup across devices — nothing else about you is collected. Your daily tasks, notes, and check-ins stay only on your own device and are never sent anywhere. Your data is never sold or shared with anyone.
